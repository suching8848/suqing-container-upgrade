用布、皮革和线强化已有可穿戴容器，保留原物品。使用原生“蘇清”制作分类和快捷入口，无自建菜单或额外等级悬浮区。本版不含EX。
支持172个精确ID：原版84、模组88；完整分类191项另含1项仅还原及18项排除。8条指定原生尺寸转换保留有效记录。来源模组为可选适配，不附带第三方资源。

[h3]档位与用法[/h3]
容量/减重：I 12/90%，II 20/93%，III 30/95%，IV 40/97%，V最高49/99%。高值保留，双项已达标则跳档，同件只允许下一档。重包安全容量可能46–48；原生高容量包只升减重，浣熊市两件保留沙盒与特性算法。
每档布/皮革/线用量：4/0/2、6/2/4、10/4/6、14/6/8、20/10/10；裁缝0–4级。针保留，满卷普通线20用量，表中不是线轴数。
卸下、放主物品栏、清空物品与全部液体后操作；储水升数不变。旧档位即使亮起也会拒绝且不扣料；动画结束不代表成功。前后换位先清空目标槽，不挤掉另一包。
保留外观、自重、槽位、挂点、耐久、其他ModData、换弹与自动装弹逻辑。胸挂尺寸、弹带过滤、肩套最多2个手枪弹匣的限制保留。其他新制作配方不保证继承强化，消耗前先还原。
[h3]还原与范围[/h3]
成功还原首次规格，返有效账本累计真实实付材料50%，各类分别向下取整；线按用量返，可得部分线轴。全付I–V为54布/22皮/30线，返27/11/15。免费、跳档、未扣料不记账，旧无账付款不追溯；成功后清账防重复。停用不会自动还原保存属性。
踏行千山与原版粗制皮/防水布包沿用原生升级；踏行千山五仅还原。传奇背包10色与Estate39的Miku包不收费强化；非穿戴箱子不支持。
维护者确认v1.0.0个人单人使用正常。新增四项本轮仅离线检查，尚待游戏内验收；172项不是全部逐件、所有组合或保存重进均已验证。

[h2]English[/h2]
[b]Greatly changes realism and balance; add with care. Intended for collectors and stockpilers. B42.21 single-player only; multiplayer and dedicated servers are unvalidated.[/b]
Upgrade existing wearable containers with cloth, leather and thread. Keeps the original item and uses the native “蘇清” crafting category/quick entry; no custom menu or extra tier tooltip area. No EX upgrades.
Supports 172 exact IDs: 84 vanilla + 88 mod items. The 191-entry classification also has 1 restore-only entry and 18 exclusions. Eight audited native size conversions preserve valid records. Source mods are optional integrations; third-party assets are not included.
[h3]Added in 1.0.1[/h3]
CombatStims.InjectorCase, CodexCBRT.SST02_ChestRig, ESHF.Qwe_Backpack and ESHF.Qwe_BackpackFront.
Retains injector filtering, maximum item size 10, front/back waist-pack slot protection and valid existing records. Extra Adrenapen storage and HavenFall large-backpack EX remain separate extensions.
[h3]Tiers and use[/h3]
Capacity/weight reduction: I 12/90%, II 20/93%, III 30/95%, IV 40/97%, V up to 49/99%. Higher values stay; tiers already met by both values are skipped. Only the next tier is allowed. Heavy-bag safety limits may yield 46–48. Specified native high-capacity bags receive weight reduction only; the two Raccoon City containers retain sandbox/trait calculations.
Per-tier cloth/leather/thread uses: 4/0/2, 6/2/4, 10/4/6, 14/6/8, 20/10/10; Tailoring 0–4. Needle retained. A full ordinary thread spool has 20 uses; these are not spool counts.
Unequip, put in main inventory, empty items and drain all fluids; water capacity stays unchanged. Old lit-up tiers are rejected without payment; animation completion alone is not success. Clear the destination slot before swapping; another pack is not displaced.
Keeps appearance, weight, slots, attachments, condition, other ModData and reload/automatic-loading logic. Rig size limits, ammunition-belt filters and the shoulder holster's 2-pistol-magazine cap remain. Other crafting recipes need not preserve upgrades; restore bags before consuming them.
[h3]Restoration and scope[/h3]
Restoring the first specification refunds 50% of each material's actual cumulative ledger payment, rounded down separately. Thread returns in uses, possibly as a partial spool. Paying I–V costs 54 cloth/22 leather/30 thread uses and refunds 27/11/15. Free/skipped/unconsumed operations are unrecorded; older unrecorded payments are not reconstructed. Success clears the ledger to prevent repeats. Disabling the mod does not restore saved attributes.
踏行千山 and vanilla crude leather/tarp bags keep their native upgrade chains; 踏行千山五 is restore-only. Ten Legendary Backpack colors and Estate39's Miku bag are excluded from paid upgrades. Non-wearable crates are unsupported.
The maintainer confirmed normal personal single-player use of v1.0.0. The four additions have offline checks this round and await gameplay acceptance. 172 is not a claim that every item, combination or save/reload scenario was tested.
